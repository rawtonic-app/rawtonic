Changes to the spektrafilm LUTs shipped with Rawtonic
=====================================================

The LUTs in this folder are by Andrea Volpato (spektrafilm,
https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) under CC BY-SA 4.0
(SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt). Their data is unchanged; changes are listed
below.

2026-10-08: files renamed to neutral display names.

2026-10-11: the header lines naming the spektrafilm bundle, film stock,
print stock and bundle file were replaced by one "Source" line, and the
TITLE by the display name, because they named other companies' film
products. Each file is one of the six one-LUT bundles (V-Log to sRGB,
25^3) of spektrafilm 0.3.4. The LUT data is unchanged.
