"Rawtonic" and the Rawtonic logo identify this project. Forks and
modified builds are welcome under the AGPL, but please give them a
different name and icon so people can tell them apart from Rawtonic's own
releases. You may say a fork is "based on Rawtonic".

Other names used in this repository and in the app are trademarks of
their owners, for example Adobe, Camera Raw, Lightroom and DNG (Adobe),
Capture One (Capture One A/S), Apple ProRAW and Display P3 (Apple),
Adobe RGB (Adobe), ProPhoto RGB (Eastman Kodak Company), X-Trans (Fujifilm), and the camera makers' names. Rawtonic uses
them only to name those products, for example the catalogs, sessions,
presets, colour spaces, file formats and sensor layouts it can read or
write, or software it was compared with.
Rawtonic is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any of them.

C2PA names the open provenance standard Rawtonic writes manifests in
(Export > Provenance (C2PA)); Rawtonic is not a C2PA member and its
manifests are not conformance-tested or trust-listed. The app does not use
the terms "Content Credentials" or the Content Credentials icon: their usage
terms were not found published (checked 2026-11-21), so the feature is named
descriptively.

Contributors: do not give Rawtonic's own tools, presets, looks or profiles
the names of other companies' products (no film stock or film
simulation names, no other editors' look or profile names); describe what
they do instead.
