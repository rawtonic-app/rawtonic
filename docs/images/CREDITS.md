## Retained captures — Pexels

`readme-export.jpg` remains the 0.1.0-alpha.1 capture (October 11, 2026); `readme-masks.jpg`, `readme-retouch.jpg` and `readme-relight.jpg` remain the 0.1.0-alpha.2 captures (October 16, 2026). They were made in a disposable showcase session and retained because the alpha.7 website captures do not show the same mask, portrait retouch, relight or export-recipe views. The Relight screenshot shows the golden hour portrait with one added cool light from the right (intensity 100, colour #a8c8ff) and the original light at 30, in the before/after split at 50 %. They show JPEG photographs, not RAW comparison results. The retouch screenshot shows Rawtonic's automatic face retouch (Blemishes 40, Even skin 20, Dark circles 25) on the studio portrait as rendered by the app; the photographs themselves were not otherwise edited. The source photographs in these retained captures are used under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/); they are not covered by the repository's software license. The photographers do not endorse Rawtonic.
