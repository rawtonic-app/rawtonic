# README photography and screenshots

The `readme-*.jpg` images are genuine window captures of Rawtonic on macOS, made in a disposable showcase session: Library and Export with 0.1.0-alpha.1 (October 11, 2026); Develop, Color, Masks, Retouch, Multi view and Relight with 0.1.0-alpha.2 (October 16, 2026). The Relight screenshot shows one added light (intensity 80) with the original light at 35 in the before/after split. They show JPEG photographs, not RAW comparison results. The retouch screenshot shows Rawtonic's automatic face retouch (Blemishes 40, Even skin 20, Dark circles 25) on the studio portrait as rendered by the app; the photographs themselves were not otherwise edited. The source photographs are used under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/); they are not covered by the repository's software license. The photographers do not endorse Rawtonic.
