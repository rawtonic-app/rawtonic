# README photography and screenshots

The README images are genuine window captures of Rawtonic on macOS. Their photographic content has separate licenses from the repository’s software. The photographers do not endorse Rawtonic.

## November 17, 2026 captures — pixls.us PlayRaw

`readme-develop.jpg`, `readme-library.jpg`, `readme-color.jpg`, `readme-multiview.jpg` and `readme-filmstrip.jpg` were captured on November 17, 2026 with 0.1.0-alpha.16 plus the changes merged up to commit `dbd2b4eb` (a QA build of `main`), in a copy of the demo session used for the alpha.7 website captures. They are window captures made by the app itself at 2880 × 1800 backing pixels, cropped where noted and scaled to 1440 pixels wide, with nothing painted or retouched afterwards: `readme-library.jpg` is the top quarter of the window, `readme-color.jpg` a band through the Color grading panel and the photo, `readme-multiview.jpg` the window below its title bar with the side panels and filmstrip hidden. The photographs were developed in Rawtonic (the demo session's edits, unchanged); the filmstrip and multi view show only the eleven photographs of the session's album "Best of the year", listed below. These screenshots are adaptations shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Their photographs, including filmstrip thumbnails, are credited below; display filenames are chosen for the demo. The source RAW files are not included.

`readme-develop.jpg` shows Dolomites_sunset.ARW, `readme-library.jpg` the four Sony photographs (Cat_in_the_brush, Dolomites_sunset, Kitten_in_plants, Mt_Cook), `readme-color.jpg` Chania_lighthouse.CR2, `readme-filmstrip.jpg` Cat_in_the_brush.ARW with the filmstrip docked on the right, and `readme-multiview.jpg` all eleven.

## November 17, 2026 captures — Pexels

`readme-masks.jpg`, `readme-retouch.jpg`, `readme-relight.jpg` and `readme-export.jpg` were captured on November 17, 2026 with the same build (0.1.0-alpha.16 plus the changes up to commit `dbd2b4eb`), in a new disposable session ("Portrait study") into which the ten Pexels photographs below were imported unchanged, as downloaded from the source links. They replace the alpha.1 and alpha.2 captures with the same views; full window captures at 2880 × 1800 backing pixels scaled to 1440 × 900, with nothing painted or retouched afterwards. The Masks screenshot shows a one-click Subject layer on the golden hour portrait (layer Exposure +0.3) with the mask overlay on. The Retouch screenshot shows Rawtonic's automatic face retouch (Blemishes 40, Even skin 20, Dark circles 25; the app reports 8 blemishes healed) on the studio portrait at 100 % zoom. The Relight screenshot shows the golden hour portrait with the Side light pattern added from the right, in the before/after split at 50 %. The Export screenshot shows the dialog over the studio portrait with three recipes ticked (High Quality, Fast (Web) and a Print TIFF recipe: TIFF, 16 bit, Adobe RGB). They show JPEG photographs, not RAW comparison results. The source photographs are used under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/); they are not covered by the repository's software license. The photographers do not endorse Rawtonic.
